# 📄 Malik (MLK) Protocol — Technical Whitepaper v1.0

## 1. Executive Summary
Cross-border currency conversion and crypto-to-fiat settlements suffer from high fees, slow banking delays, and fragmented liquidity. **Malik (MLK)** solves this by introducing a unified, dollar-pegged universal bridge asset (**1 MLK = $1.00 USD**) capable of routing value across **150+ global fiat currencies** and major cryptocurrencies in seconds.

## 2. Tokenomics & Valuation Standard
- **Token Name:** Malik
- **Ticker Symbol:** MLK
- **Precision:** 18 Decimals (supporting micro-transactions down to $0.000001)
- **Peg Standard:** `1 MLK = $1.00 USD`
- **Initial Genesis Supply:** 100,000,000 MLK

## 3. Architecture & Core Smart Contracts
1. **MalikToken (`ERC-20`):** Core asset with built-in AML blacklisting and emergency pause controls.
2. **MalikBridge:** Automated multi-currency conversion router enforcing a strict **$10,000 daily per-wallet limit**.
3. **MalikLiquidityPool:** Decentralized reserve pool enabling instant liquidity settlements.
4. **MalikOracle:** Tamper-proof rate verification layer ensuring real-time accuracy with a 1-hour staleness protection lock.

## 4. Regulatory & AML Compliance Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Current):** Open-source prototype, smart contract suite, and live Web3 rate converter.
- **Phase 2:** Testnet deployment (Polygon Amoy) and ecosystem developer grants.
- **Phase 3:** Full commercial launch with mandatory KYC, FIU-IND registration (India), and licensed fiat on/off-ramp banking partners.
