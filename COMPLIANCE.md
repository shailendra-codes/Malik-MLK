# 🛡️ Malik (MLK) AML & Regulatory Compliance Policy

**Malik Protocol** enforces a strict **Zero-Tolerance Policy** against illegal funds, money laundering, and financial fraud.

## 1. Mandatory KYC (Know Your Customer)
- All fiat-to-MLK and MLK-to-fiat conversions require verified government identity checks (Aadhaar/PAN in India, Passport/National ID globally).
- Anonymous cash transactions are strictly prohibited.

## 2. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Controls
- **Daily Transaction Cap:** Standard wallets are restricted to **$10,000 USD (10,000 MLK)** per 24 hours via smart contract enforcement.
- **On-Chain Blacklisting:** Wallets linked to hacks, scams, or sanctioned entities are immediately blocked via `blacklistAddress()` in `MalikToken.sol`.

## 3. Global & Indian Regulatory Alignment
- Designed to comply with **FIU-IND (Financial Intelligence Unit - India)** guidelines and global FATF travel rules.
